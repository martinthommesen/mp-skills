# Native question tools: previous rejection superseded

The previous rejection of native question tools is superseded by the eligible-tool policy in [invocation guidance](../.agents/invocation.md#live-questions) and the standalone [grilling instructions](../skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md). Grilling uses the current host's exposed question tool when its schema permits the request, one focused question at a time. Otherwise it asks one concise question in chat.

The current schema decides eligibility, choice or freeform inputs, and handling of skipped optional preferences. A required answer remains pending until the human responds. Learning assessments preserve recall by suggesting no correct answer. Generated questionnaires and lesson quizzes retain their own artifact formats.

This change does not require a particular product or question UI. Batched interviews and mandatory HTML questionnaire modes remain outside the one-ready-question grilling workflow.
